# Pollutant sequence v3: training risk-weighted loss

The v2 MSE-only fixed ensemble failed the preregistered gate against v1: Poor+ misses rose from 111 to 133 of 248 pollution diagnostic hours and from 12 to 13 of 16 later hours. False alarms fell from 41 to 32 and from one to zero, respectively, but Very Poor+ misses remained eleven of eleven. Its ozone tail underprediction also worsened. Reject v2 as the next incumbent; retain the fixed v1 ensemble for research comparisons.

This study makes **one change relative to v1**: retain standardized SmoothL1 and weight a training origin's loss by two if its actual final-sixth-hour canonical rounded category is Poor or worse, otherwise by one. Apply that origin weight to all six pollutants and six horizons. Normalize the batch loss by the sum of origin weights, so weighting does not simply double the global loss scale. Development MAE is unweighted, and checkpoint selection remains unchanged. The multiplier two is fixed before this new run; no diagnostic threshold or multiplier sweep is allowed.

Training has 296 Poor+ origins among 19,663 eligible origins. Only five are Very Poor+ in one episode. The hypothesis is that a modest explicit training priority may improve risk recall without the v2 squared-loss regression. Correlated training hours are not independent events, and this weighting does not create new extreme examples or cure the missing official Severe class. It may raise false alarms or overfit one rare episode, which the unchanged paired guardrails must expose.

Compute weights **only from training targets**, using the same canonical category rounding as evaluation. No validation or diagnostic weights enter fitting. Future labels are supervised training targets only; actual future weather/pollutants never become predictor inputs. All other data, phase bounds, support eligibility, preprocessing, causal architecture, three seeds, optimizer, maximum 60 epochs, patience eight, clipping and checkpoint rules stay fixed under the [v1 protocol](DEEP_POLLUTANT_STUDY_PROTOCOL.md).

The primary reference remains the SHA-pinned completed **v1 fixed concentration-average ensemble**, not v2 or a selected seed.
