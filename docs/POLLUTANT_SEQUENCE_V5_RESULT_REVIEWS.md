# V5 measured-result reviews

Three read-only research reviewers examined the completed V5 raw result, matched V3 reference, report, training curves, V4 findings and lag probe. Result integrity was independently checked.

- **Model and architecture:** V5 does not support another immediate fit, longer training, or a larger model. The fixed mean's accuracy was nearly unchanged in pollution (0.790698 vs 0.791172), but Poor+ misses increased 88 to 94 and later-season misses increased 6 to 10. Pollution false alarms fell and two additional Poor+ episodes had any hit, but later episode hits decreased from four to two. V5 missed all 11 pollution Very Poor+ hours. The endpoint weighted development score bottomed at epochs 18, 19 and 16, then worsened; more epochs are not supported.
- **Data and error review:** training still has 296 Poor+ origins in 86 correlated episodes and only five Very Poor+ hours in one episode. The context probe and V5 provide no case for more history, larger capacity or loss sweeps. Recommend attributing development misses and then seeking additional independent, station aligned high-pollution episodes.
- **Evaluation and integrity:** all source/reference hashes, ordered timestamps, actual pollutants, canonical labels, support and deterministic baselines match V3. The failure is a measured model outcome, not a pairing defect. All three seeds and fixed ensemble fail the unchanged V3 gate. Zero-support recall remains UNMEASURED.

The requested saved-output attribution is now in eval/raw/pollutant_sequence_v5_error_analysis.md. It shows every development Poor+ hour was ozone controlled; V5 missed 70/168 versus V3's 62/168, and ozone MAE worsened from 21.86 to 22.88 with bias -20.14 to -21.66. In the pollution diagnostic, 247/248 Poor+ hours were ozone controlled; V5 missed 94 versus 88. These exploratory files do not establish that ozone is the only driver outside these consumed rows or that an ozone-specific model change will generalize.

**Decision:** reject V5 as incumbent and do not start another fit on the same consumed modeled archive. The next useful evidence is additional quality controlled, station aligned high-pollution episodes with timestamps and units, especially recurring Very Poor and official Severe events; current training has one Very Poor episode and no official Severe samples. Keep official Severe recall UNMEASURED. No global model maximum is claimed, and a transformer has no evidence-based case here.
