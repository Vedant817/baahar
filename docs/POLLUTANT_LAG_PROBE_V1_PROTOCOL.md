# Fixed older-history signal probe, v1

The completed support audit establishes that 48h histories are feasible, not that they help. The three reviews differ about immediately fitting another TCN. This experiment resolves the specific missing evidence with a cheap, fixed linear comparison on training and consumed development only. It is a distinct study, not a rerun of v1-v4. It does not alter any diagnostic threshold or historical gate.

Use the source-pinned archive and exact audited 48h-eligible origins for both candidates: 19,639 training and 1,411 development origins. Wholly phase-local six-gas history t-47..t and target t+6 must exist and be finite. Verify both origin SHA digests against the completed support audit. Train is 2023-01-01 to 2025-04-01; development is 2025-04-01 to 2025-06-01, exclusive ends. The history/target eligibility excludes boundary overlap. No 2026 diagnostic labels enter fitting, evaluation, or selection.

Baseline: flatten recent t-23..t six-gas, five observed-weather, and four calendar channels. Extended: prepend older t-47..t-24 six-gas and five observed-weather channels to the identical baseline. Omit older calendar columns: duplicated periodic columns could change the effective Ridge penalty without adding historical source information. Missing weather receives medians fit only on training; predictor standardization and six final-hour target scales also fit only on training. Future weather is never an input. Both models predict six final-hour concentrations using fixed Ridge alpha=10, Cholesky solver, identical train Poor+ origin weight two (other origins one), and no tuning or seed selection. Canonical NAQI comes from nonnegative-clipped concentrations under the pinned decoder. Report the count of negative predictions before clipping.

Report overall and April/May development normalized MAE (mean absolute error divided by train target standard deviations), each pollutant MAE, category accuracy, Poor+/Very Poor+/official Severe misses, precision, false alarms and episode hits. Save actual/predicted development concentrations and origin times. This probe has no probability outputs; unsupported recall remains null/UNMEASURED.

Predeclared evidence filter for considering one matched 48h TCN study: extended overall normalized MAE improves at least 1%; neither April nor May normalized MAE worsens; Poor+ and Very Poor+ misses and false alarms do not increase; Poor+ any-hit episode count does not decrease. All conditions must pass. This is an adaptive research screen, not a statistical significance test or the existing neural diagnostic gate. Failure does not disprove nonlinear older-history signal. Passing justifies consideration of a matched 24h/48h TCN comparison; it does not automatically authorize promotion or change product safety.

One CPU-only Modal call, timeout 1800 seconds, retries zero, no local weights/PyTorch. Source pin runner, v3 helper implementation, NAQI code, support result and this protocol; pin source archive fixtures and row SHA. Existing manifest prevents duplicate submission; fetched raw result is immutable. All data are consumed CAMS/ERA5 modeled archive, with limited rare-event training support, not station observations, pristine holdouts, prospective evidence or medical validation.

Method references: [scikit-learn Ridge](https://scikit-learn.org/1.6/modules/generated/sklearn.linear_model.Ridge.html), [train-only preprocessing](https://scikit-learn.org/1.6/common_pitfalls.html#data-leakage), [chronological splits with a gap](https://scikit-learn.org/1.6/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.TimeSeriesSplit.html).
